# AI STATEMENT

This document describes how this project uses Artificial Intelligence.

## Vision

Artificial Intelligence, especially generative Artificial Intelligence, has become the defining technology of the current era as of the creation of this project, despite still being in its early stages. It has dominated the discourse and become a loaded term which all companies venerate and most of the population is suspicious of. As a technology, AI is essentially neutral, its impact being determined by how it is used.

This project is developed with the conviction that we can only steer Artificial Intelligence towards a positive impact by actively using it to create positive examples. In doing so, we make the future we envision a shared possibility. By refusing to engage with this technology, we would completely hand over the paving of the way forward to those whom we cannot control, and whose minds we cannot know.

AI is used in this project in ways that are fundamentally beneficial to its users. It is used to make this project economically viable and reactive to the player's intentions in ways that would not be possible without it. More details about each of these cases in the sections below.

## Usage

### AI in development

As with most software at the time of its creation, this project is developed with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence. The activity in which AI is most used is code writing, although AI is also used at times for planning, debugging and writing documentation.

AI makes this project economically viable. Not because it reduces costs and raises profit margins (in fact, this project is distributed free of charge), but because it accelerates the implementation of the vision designed for the project. Because of this, a project which would have taken too long to be considered feasible can be done in a timeframe and with a level of demand for resources that a single developer can provide.

At its best, by reducing the cost (mostly in terms of time, not merely money) of experimentation, AI allows for ideas that do not have a consolidated market fit to be explored and tested out, enabling new creative possibilities. This project is a game that has at its core mechanics, systems and goals that are not widely used and validated, and, as such, it represents the exact type of exploration that AI makes more justifiable.

### AI as end product

This project includes AI-generated content as part of its end product, with which the user interacts directly. At a minor level, this includes the UI and most of the menu text; at a major level, this includes the text of events, questions and answers, and content from all NPCs (councilors, adversaries and influencers), as well as suggestions for the player actions and speeches.

The intent of using AI in the way it is used here is to provide an experience that truly reacts to the player. By putting generative AI at the core of the game's mechanics and in-game content generation, the sandbox nature of the game is deeply enhanced, allowing the player to shape and steer their experience in almost any way they can imagine, much further than what could be provided with pre-designed answers and settings.

Local AI, running on the player's own hardware, is the option this project fully endorses, first and foremost. Local inference keeps everything generated during a campaign (every speech, answer and event) on the player's machine and under the player's control, and it is the reason this project treats a local engine as its preferred option. At the same time, local inference demands hardware that not everyone has access to. For those players, the project also supports a hosted alternative, OpenRouter, as a deliberate trade-off: the player chooses to share their gameplay data with a cloud provider in exchange for access to models that can deliver the experience they are looking for. What matters is that the choice always belongs to the player: which engine to use, which model to run, and whether anything leaves their machine at all.

There is no substitute for human creativity when it comes to shaping narrative progression and stories. Any attempt to use AI as a cheaper replacement for a human storyteller, at least with the current state of the technology, will inevitably fail. Even if the technology improves to the point in which it can exceed a human's level of quality in narration, human design will always take center stage when it comes to our collective imagination. What AI brings, and what is explored in this project, is new ways to provide a sandbox that people can enter and enjoy a highly customized experience that can be taken wherever the person's imagination can go, at a scale that was impossible before. This project is intended to be a positive example of what can be done in this direction.
